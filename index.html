Scoring is the Universo BSB Fantasy league's: triple-double, steal, block, 40+ point game and 50+ point game +2 each; double-double, assist, 15+ assist game and 20+ rebound game +1 each; rebound +0.75; point and 3-pointer made +0.5 each; turnover −1; technical and flagrant foul −2 each. A triple-double also counts as a double-double. Points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, turnovers and 3-pointers made are per-game averages; the rest are season totals.
